these are explicit rows.

HOW TO READ THE VERDICT COLUMN. AGREE means the two engines answered the variant identically -
which also tells you this port's answer to it, because agreement is equality. DIVERGE means they
did not and no entry in tests/FuzzyRegex.OracleTests/ExpectedDivergences.cs claims that question.
Anything else is the id of the entry that does.

THE `prefilter-on` / `prefilter-off` ROWS ARE A MEASUREMENT AND NOT AN ARTEFACT, which is worth
saying because for one revision of the script they were not: upstream's required-string prefilter
is switched by the recorder's `generator` TAG (tools/record-oracle.py:325 and :522), not by the
`oracle` field, which the recorder derives and writes back. Setting `oracle` produced 22 variants
that were byte-identical to their base rows. They flip the tag now, and every one of them still
reads `same` - so on these rows the prefilter genuinely changes nothing.

THE DIVERGING VARIANTS ARE NOT UNFINISHED WORK. An ablation is a DIFFERENT question from the row
it came from, so a variant that diverges is a question no wave has drawn and no entry was written
for. What S52 owed was the 37 rows of the sweep itself, and the replay of those is
`expected 37, diverge 0`:

    pwsh -File tools/run-oracle.ps1 -Rows tools/probes/sweep-divergence-rows.jsonl
